# governance-lab research radar - 2026-10-25

Weekly scan of the frontier for governance primitives genuinely worth building,
and a note on what cleared the quality bar this week and why.

## The frontier this week

Signal we tracked and what it tells us about an unmet need. Nine items; the two
that pulled the batch into their orbit are called out in "What we chose to build."

1. **arXiv 2602.10453, *The Landscape of Prompt Injection Threats in LLM Agents:
   From Taxonomy to Defenses*** - the current reference taxonomy of injection
   threats, class-by-class. Reinforces that the gap is not in detection but in
   provenance labelling: the model cannot tell what in its context came from an
   adversarial surface.

2. **arXiv 2607.13149, *Composable Trust for Language Models*** - argues for a
   composable trust layer with explicit content blocks and labels. Converges on
   the four-level provenance shape (trusted / observed / quarantined / untrusted).

3. **arXiv 2610.07274, *A Trust Layer for Agent Evaluation*** - names an explicit
   severity ordering of gates and the structural separation between the gate and
   the thing it gates. Reinforces the transition-matrix shape.

4. **DigitalApplied, *Prompt Injection in Production Agents: 2026 Taxonomy*** -
   industry read of 200 production agent audits. 10 attack classes, 9 of 10
   arriving through *trusted* channels (retrieved documents, tool outputs, memory
   stores, email bodies, collaborating subagents, the API responses the agent
   depends on to do its job). Maps to OWASP LLM01 and the Agentic AI Top 10 T1 /
   T6. The signal is: the ingress discipline is where the work is, not the
   downstream detector.

5. **Cloud Security Alliance Labs, *MCP Attack Surface: Tool Poisoning and IDE
   Auto-Execution* (2026-07-01 research note)** - names the specific MCP gap:
   "no envelope marking tool descriptions as data-not-instructions, no signed
   origin, no provenance attached when the client merges it with the developer's
   own system prompt." Three attack variants (description poisoning, rug-pull,
   tool shadowing) share one structural cause: trust inherited silently from
   servers without continuous verification.

6. **ICLR 2026, *FaithCoT-Bench: Benchmarking Instance-Level Faithfulness of
   Chain-of-Thought Reasoning*** - a benchmark for CoT faithfulness across model
   families and datasets. Reinforces the gap: a reasoning trace is routinely
   plausible but not causally grounded in the computation that produced the
   answer.

7. **arXiv 2603.26410, *Why Models Know But Don't Say: Chain-of-Thought
   Faithfulness Divergence Between Thinking Tokens and Answers in Open-Weight
   Reasoning Models*** - a 2026 study of the divergence between what a reasoning
   model's visible tokens claim and what the model actually used to decide.
   Reinforces that a reasoning-depth tag is a load-bearing honesty primitive, not
   a convenience.

8. **arXiv 2605.24286, *Faithfulness as Information Flow: Evaluating and Training
   Faithful Chain-of-Thought Reasoning*** - alternative framing of the same gap
   via information flow. Signal corroborates FaithCoT-Bench.

9. **arXiv 2603.22816, *Measuring and curing reasoning rigidity: from decorative
   chain-of-thought to genuine faithfulness*** - names "decorative" chain-of-
   thought and argues for an operational distinction between true and
   decorative thinking steps.

**Belongs elsewhere.** The downstream *detection* of injection in tool outputs
(string-level scanning, semantic injection detectors) is live frontier signal
too, but it is a runtime-enforcement concern that `agent-guard-lab` is the right
home for; this lab stays at the shape-and-discipline layer. The sibling boundary
is in each lab's charter.

## What we chose to build this week

Two artifacts cleared the quality bar - both charter-pool items, both anchored
to multiple independent 2026 signals above.

### 1. `trustlabels` - taxonomy and transition-matrix linter

The ingress-side primitive. A nine-label taxonomy (trusted / observed /
quarantined / untrusted, each with one or two qualifiers), a seven-edge promotion
matrix that names the only valid transitions and the gate each requires
(quarantined to trusted-audited through a `reviewer`; untrusted-derived to
observed-tool through an `automated-sanitizer`), and twelve rules under `TL-NNN`
that refuse the real failure modes - a quarantined entry used as grounds for
action, a promotion recorded without a gate, a declared promotion the taxonomy
does not permit, a `trusted:system` entry sourced from an external URL.

Charter mapping: candidate pool item 3 (`trustlabels`). Kind: `linter`.
Signals it answers to: items 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 above - five independent 2026
convergent sources make a canonical, tested, clean-room linter the useful next
step; builds on last week's `envelope` as the ingress-side primitive that
`envelope` was missing a counterpart to.

### 2. `rungtag` - reasoning-depth-tag linter for text streams

The honesty-side primitive at the free-text layer. A parser that splits a
markdown or plain-text transcript into blocks, a tag grammar
`(TOKEN(, TOKEN)*)` with four primary tokens and a three-rung Pearl dimension,
and nine rules under `RT-NNN` that catch substantive untagged blocks, iterated
tags claimed with no checker named, rung-3 claims that omit the iterated loop
that could cause-check them, malformed tags, and overclaims on short blocks.

Charter mapping: candidate pool item 6 (`rungtag`). Kind: `linter`.
Signals it answers to: items 6, 7, 8, 9 above - four independent 2026 CoT-
faithfulness sources name the same pragmatic mitigation (a reasoning-depth tag
plus a maker-checker loop). `rungtag` is the text-layer counterpart to
`envelope`'s `ENV-002` cross-field rule, which only sees the structured-JSON
return-value surface. The two now compose at the two layers at which workers
actually write.

### Not built this week

`makerchecker`, `riskgate`, `sycoprobe`, `decisionrecord` (pool items 4, 5, 7,
8) - deferred. The pattern is intentional: this radar built the two ingress
and honesty primitives that compose directly with `envelope` and `auditledger`
from last week, bringing the lab to four composed artifacts: a return-shape
contract, an append-only ledger of what the system did, an ingress-side
provenance discipline, and a free-text reasoning-depth discipline. The deferred
items will compose on top of this base; building them without it first would
make the composition awkward.

use is possible from here. A reader looking at these primitives in the field is
